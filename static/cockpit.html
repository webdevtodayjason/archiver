What this is

Your notes, held by a machine on your desk. Nothing leaves the house. The device reads every note, works out what the recurring names are, and draws the ones that keep turning up together.

Three things to try right now

1. Click a project on the left. The field rebuilds around it.

2. Click any name in the graph. It pins to the centre and the right rail fills with where it appears.

3. Type a question in the box at the bottom right. Answers stack above it.

Is it on?

The light in the top bar. Green means the device answered, embeddings ran, and the answering model is reachable. Click it for the detail.

What the dots are

Names that appear across many notes. Size is how often. A line between two means they show up in the same notes more than chance would explain.

Moving around

hover inspect · click focus and rebuild · drag pan · scroll zoom · esc release.

Why some names are missing

The graph draws the strongest few links per name, not all of them. The full set is in the corpus. Focus a name to pull its own neighbourhood forward.

Scope

The word next to Ask is what the question is aimed at: everything, one entity, or one project. Click something to change it.

Answers cite

Every answer names the notes it came from. If nothing retrieved covers the question, it says so instead of inventing one.

Asking one note

Open a note and use the box at the bottom of that window. That reads the whole note rather than searching.

Audio overview

▶ audio overview puts two hosts on whatever the scope is and speaks it on the device. Around three and a half minutes of work for five minutes of audio, with a progress line while it runs.

Every line shows the note it came from, and clicking a line jumps the audio to it. A line the hosts could not source is deleted before it is spoken, and the count of those is shown above the script.

New notes

+ note in the top bar. Title, optional project, body. It lands in your vault as plain markdown, gets chunked and embedded, and is answerable in seconds. No import step.

Taking one with you

Open any note: copy md or download. Plain markdown, no wrapper.

From Obsidian

python3 md2jsonl.py ~/vault out.jsonl then archivist add-text out.jsonl. Folder becomes the project, filename becomes the title.

Then

archivist chunk then archivist index to embed, then archivist entities to rebuild the name index. Re-running only does the new notes.

From PDFs

archivist add *.pdf. Scanned pages go through OCR on the device.
